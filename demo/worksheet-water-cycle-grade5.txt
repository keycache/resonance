Water Cycle Worksheet - Grade 5

Read the short lesson and then complete the tasks.

The water cycle has three main stages: evaporation, condensation, and precipitation.

Evaporation happens when the sun heats water in rivers, ponds, seas, or puddles. The water changes into water vapor and rises into the air.

Condensation happens when water vapor cools in the sky and forms clouds.

Precipitation happens when clouds become heavy and water falls back to Earth as rain.

In many communities, rainwater is collected in tanks, ponds, or reservoirs and used for drinking, cooking, cleaning, and farming. During dry periods, people must use water carefully.

Tasks:
1. Draw and label the three stages of the water cycle.
2. Write two sentences explaining why rain is important for people and plants.
